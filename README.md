# ir-mettering-with-reveni-labs-SPOT-METER-mk2
The reveni labs spotmetter mk2 is a great tool who has one "flaws", an infrared sencivity(who where patched on V1.8 frimware) 


The SH0 is the "mettering curve" and the CH1 is use in v1.8 frimware to do ir cancelation 

<img width="400" alt="curve" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/989f2836-cde1-4c6f-9cc3-e2c63cdd0daa" />


beacause of the extended ir secivity, the metter can be use for ir mettering by placing the filter in front of the metter 

i have ran some bracketing to find an "ir filtered ISO" for the spot mettering 

i have tested the only 2 infrared film reaillly available on the market, the ilford sfx and agfa avipot 200 (aka rolley infrared, retro 400s, superpan 200) (i noticed that after getting each of thoos film to i decided to dev them at different spped, 100, 200 and 400 iso) 

special thanks to r/marchinmachin for giving me those film to test!!
